About me

Hi, I'm Vidhi Patra. I am a Computer Science major at Georgia Tech (Intelligence & Systems Architecture) with a FinTech Minor. I'm passionate about AI, machine learning, algorithmic trading, and building data-driven systems that make real-world impact.

My experience spans AI research internships, algorithmic trading at FinSentinal, AI engineering at Canva, and venture capital analysis. I'm also active in Georgia Tech's tech community as a Product Lead at AI@GT, developer at many Coding Clubs, and was a Campus Strategist for Perplexity.
